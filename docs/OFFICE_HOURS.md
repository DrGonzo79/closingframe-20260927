# Office Hours — ClosingFrame

Generated 2026-09-27 · Startup diagnostic · Pre-product

## Six forcing questions

1. **Demand reality:** no verified demand evidence is available. The source establishes a plausible analytics gap, not willingness to pay. Validation must start with paid design partners, not a waitlist.
2. **Painful status quo:** a brokerage social lead exports platform analytics, manually tags listings, asks agents where leads originated, and reconciles CRM/MLS outcomes in a spreadsheet. Views are easy; trustworthy revenue attribution is slow and contested.
3. **Desperate specificity:** the first customer is the marketing director at a 25–150-agent residential brokerage who must defend video spend to the broker-owner each month. Their risk is budget cuts when they cannot tie production to pipeline.
4. **Narrowest paid wedge:** one weekly evidence report for TikTok listing videos matched to one CRM’s leads and showing records. No cross-channel suite, content generator or autonomous agent in the first sale.
5. **Observation plan:** shadow three marketing directors through their monthly report. Give them an unlabeled CSV matcher, do not demo it, and watch which exceptions they correct. Success is one partner paying $500–$1,500/month and using the report in a broker review.
6. **Future-fit:** as synthetic content increases volume, reliable first-party outcome joins become more valuable. If platforms restrict identifiers, the product becomes less viable unless brokerage-owned links and CRM capture are the primary identity spine.

## Premise challenge

- The problem is not “better social analytics”; it is defensible budget allocation.
- A view-to-close claim can create false confidence. The product must expose evidence, competing touchpoints and uncertainty.
- The wedge works only where listings, content and downstream events share stable identifiers.

## Approaches considered

### A — Weekly CSV matcher (minimal viable)
Effort S · Risk Low. Upload platform and CRM exports, receive an exception-aware report. Fastest paid test; manual and not real-time.

### B — Evidence-led attribution workspace (recommended)
Effort M · Risk Medium. Connect one platform and CRM, retain inspectable joins, rank video patterns and generate broker-ready reports. Enough repeat value without pretending to be a full marketing cloud.

### C — Brokerage growth operating system (ideal architecture)
Effort XL · Risk High. Multi-platform ingestion, warehouse, identity graph, experimentation and autonomous recommendations. Strong destination, far too broad before demand and data access are proven.

## Recommendation and assignment

Build B as a thin vertical slice while selling A manually. Within seven days, recruit three brokerage marketing directors and ask each for last month’s video export plus a redacted pipeline export. Charge for one reconciled report; measure exception rate and whether the report changes the next content brief.
